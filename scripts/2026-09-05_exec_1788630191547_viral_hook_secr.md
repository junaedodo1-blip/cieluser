## Slide 2 (problem)
**Header**: why 90% of videos die in the first 2 seconds.
**Subhead**: Starting with boring greetings ("Hey guys") or channel logos kills distribution instantly.
**Badge**: THE RETENTION TRAP
**Callout**: RULE: Never introduce yourself. Start right inside the middle of the transformation.
**Bullets**:
- The algorithm decides in 1.5 to 3 seconds whether to push your video
- Throat-clearing and slow intros cause 80% immediate drop-off
- Viewers want the prize immediately, not your autobiography

## Slide 3 (step1)
**Header**: layer 1: the silent visual shock.
**Subhead**: Your video must stop thumbs on mute before the viewer hears a single word.
**Badge**: LAYER 01: VISUAL SHOCK
**Callout**: VISUAL RULE: The first frame must make sense and look stunning even on mute.
**Bullets**:
- Show an impossible, oversized, or high-contrast physical object
- Use extreme close-up macro texture (water droplets, raking light, liquid splash)
- When the first frame looks visually shocking, the thumb freezes

## Slide 4 (step2)
**Header**: layer 2: the spoken intrigue (under 2s).
**Subhead**: Lead with the shocking prize or counter-intuitive truth.
**Badge**: LAYER 02: SPOKEN INTRIGUE
**Callout**: VERBAL RULE: Lead with the prize, withhold the answer until slide 3.
**Bullets**:
- Bad: "Today I want to show you how to edit videos faster."
- Good: "I spent $10,000 on video gear so you can copy the $0 formula."
- Open a curiosity gap that the viewer MUST watch 15s to resolve

## Slide 5 (step3)
**Header**: layer 3: the 4-word on-screen punchline.
**Subhead**: Do not repeat the spoken words. Sharpen the message into 4 big bold words.
**Badge**: LAYER 03: ON-SCREEN PUNCH
**Callout**: TEXT RULE: 3 to 7 high-impact words that punch the message into their eyes.
**Bullets**:
- Spoken: "Your expensive camera is why your videos look worse."
- On-screen text: "$3,000 vs $0 Truth."
- High contrast typography with a neon highlighter strip behind the keyword
