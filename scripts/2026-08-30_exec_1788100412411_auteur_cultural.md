## Slide 1 (hook)
**Header**: When anyone can generate pixels, taste is the only moat.
**Subhead**: AI made visual generation free. It made discerning art direction priceless.
**Badge**: BRAND PHILOSOPHY

## Slide 2 (problem)
**Header**: The commodity deluge.
**Subhead**: The internet is flooded with generic, glossy, synthetic AI content.
**Badge**: THE PROBLEM
**Callout**: COMMODITY LAW: When supply approaches infinity, discernment becomes the only luxury.
**Bullets**:
- When anyone can prompt a 3D render in 5 seconds, renders lose all value
- Audiences developed instant subconscious blindness to generic AI aesthetics
- What people crave today is tactile friction, material weight, and auteur vision

## Slide 3 (step1)
**Header**: Pillar 1: Physical Materiality.
**Subhead**: Anchor digital visuals into authentic physical sensory textures:
**Badge**: SECRET 1
**Callout**: RULE 1: If it looks like plastic 3D, it is forgotten. If it feels heavy, it commands respect.
**Bullets**:
- Fluted crystal glass refractions, tailored heavyweight wool, brushed titanium
- Natural imperfect lighting that embraces deep shadow wells
- Lived-in skin textures and authentic 35mm film grain

## Slide 4 (step2)
**Header**: Pillar 2: Extreme Restraint.
**Subhead**: Amateurs add more elements; masters remove until nothing more can be taken away:
**Badge**: SECRET 2
**Callout**: AUTEUR LAW: Luxury is not what you add; it is what you have the discipline to withhold.
**Bullets**:
- One dominant visual symbol or subject per composition
- Generous Abyss Black negative space that lets the subject command the room
- Restrained typography: crisp white editorial serif over photographic tone

## Slide 5 (step3)
**Header**: Pillar 3: Cultural Narrative.
**Subhead**: Every visual must carry an unspoken story and cultural point of view:
**Badge**: SECRET 3
**Callout**: BRAND APHORISM: Products become obsolete. Cultural stories become mythology.
**Bullets**:
- Do not sell product specs; broadcast a cultural philosophy
- Align your brand with timeless human values: craft, courage, transformation
- Create artifacts that people want to save, study, and pass on
