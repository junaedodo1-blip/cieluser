# 🎬 Viral Short-Form Video Storyboard
## "How I Made a $10,000 Product Ad For $0 With Google Omni" (Project Ciel - Nothing Headphones)

---

## 📌 Production Overview
* **Target Platforms:** Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Twitter/X Native Video.
* **Format & Aspect Ratio:** 9:16 Vertical (`1080 x 1920`), 30 FPS, 20 Seconds (600 Frames).
* **Visual Aesthetic:** 
  * Clean **White Background (`#FFFFFF`)** with subtle pastel gradient orbs and 36px dot grid texture.
  * **Central Media Box:** Video clips and code terminals are contained inside a central white framed box with rounded corners (`32px`) and subtle drop shadows.
  * **Outside Box Assets:** Giant bold typography (`68px`) and 2.5D brand logos placed strictly in the top/bottom safe zones (middle 75% height).
  * **Visual Explainers:** Floating step pill badges (*"✨ STEP 1: PROMPT"*, *"🚀 STEP 2: RENDER"*) and callout cards (*"💡 Explainer: ..."*).
