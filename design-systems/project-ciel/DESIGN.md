## 1. Manifesto & Narrative Doctrine

> *"Anyone can generate pixels. Today, AI makes spectacle easy. But spectacle without soul is forgotten in three seconds. Story is what makes physical objects immortal."*

### The Philosophy: Story as Soul, Visuals as Vessel
Physical objects are not transactional commodities. A luxury perfume holds the memory of twilight and rain; a sculpted garment carries armor and human identity; a skincare formulation is an intimate ritual. Traditional advertising reduces these worlds to flat, uninspired product shots. `project\ciel` operates on the principle that **Story is the only true commercial moat**.

### The 3-Act Narrative Spine
1. **Act I: The Truth (Unspoken Human Tension)**: Open on a cultural friction, curiosity gap, or vulnerable reality that the audience instinctively recognizes.
2. **Act II: The Tension (Sensory Struggle & Float)**: The internal longing, sensory discovery, zero-G suspension, or transformation of the protagonist.
3. **Act III: The Resolve (The Physical Anchor)**: The physical object lands as the emotional catalyst, identity marker, and undeniable resolution.

### Kallaway's 6 Levels of Brand Storytelling
- **Level 6 (The Maestro)**: Full Narrative Worldbuilding & Cultural Gravity. The brand feels legendary and untouchable.
- **Level 5 (The Translator)**: Translating subconscious human emotion & identity into cinematic sensory films.
- **Level 4 (The Architect)**: Systematic consistency across reels, carousels, and lookbooks.
- **Level 3 (The Champion)**: Used selectively in Remotion dynamic ad variations.
- **Level 2 (The Illusionist)**: *STRICTLY REJECTED* — We never use visual effects without emotional purpose.
- **Level 1 (The Reporter)**: Restricted exclusively to subtle Swiss HUD metadata.

## 5. The 4 Laws of Physical Product Directing

1. **Sensory Physics First**: Prioritize physical materiality — perfume mist diffusion, liquid viscosity, fabric grain, and glass refraction.
2. **Uncanny Juxtaposition**: Place impossible luxury and elegance inside raw, authentic environments (e.g. high-fashion in a raw concrete subway).
3. **Non-Uniform Pacing**: Pacing breathes with human emotion — high-velocity sprints for tension, suspended zero-G float for realization.
4. **Deliberate Sonic Cuts**: Music drops hit on physics shifts (zero-G launches), cutting to dead silence on impact to let the brand mark land.

## 7. Multi-Angle Testing & "Double-Down" Scaling Rule

> **The Optimization Doctrine**: Never rely on a single hook formula. Continuously test diverse angles, psychological hooks, and visual methods across every post cycle. When an angle demonstrates outlier engagement (high save rate, DM trigger velocity, or shares), double down on that winning angle!

### The 7 Rotating Hook Angles
1. **The Contrarian Truth** (`contrarian_truth`): Challenging common industry misconceptions with undeniable authority.
2. **The Auteur Case Study** (`case_study_directing`): Deconstructing how iconic directors & luxury houses (Glazer, Jonze, Jacquemus) command attention.
3. **The Technical Blueprint** (`technical_formula`): Providing exact code, spring math, and lighting formulas.
4. **Before vs After / Comparison** (`before_vs_after`): Highlighting the stark contrast between cheap Level 2 AI tricks and Level 6 Maestro worldbuilding.
5. **Step-by-Step Anatomy** (`step_by_step_anatomy`): Methodical breakdown of the 8-stage visual literature funnel.
6. **Uncanny Juxtaposition** (`curiosity_juxtaposition`): Exploring pattern interruption by anchoring impossible luxury inside raw brutalist environments.
7. **Commerce & Conversion Engine** (`commerce_conversion`): Demonstrating how to monetize attention automatically 24/7.

### The Double-Down Rule
- Keep testing the 7 diverse angles in round-robin rotation.
- When an angle generates high engagement or comment velocity, increment its selection weighting to produce subsequent iterations and sequels on the winning format.

## 9. The 9-Year-Old Clarity & Punchy Hook Testing Doctrine

> **The Rule of Radical Simplicity**: A great post must be understood instantly by a 9-year-old. Zero academic jargon, zero confusing buzzwords. Every hook must be punchy, unignorable, and crystal clear.

### Core Guidelines:
1. **Unignorable First Hook**: Slide 1 headline must punch through the noise in 3 words or a shocking simple statement (*"Why 99% of AI videos get skipped immediately"*, *"How giant brands get millions of views with crazy simple ideas"*).
2. **Keep Testing Angles**: Never settle on one hook style. Continuously test Contrarian Truths, Behind-the-Scenes Secrets, Crazy Juxtapositions, Before/Afters, and Simple Step-by-Step guides until an outlier hits, then double down!
3. **Plain English Explanations**:
   - Use vivid, simple metaphors (e.g. *"Water splashes so juicy you can almost taste them"*, *"Objects with real weight and bounce instead of robot glides"*).
   - Short, conversational sentences.
   - Clear 1-word comment trigger (*"Comment 'CIEL' for the free guide"*).
