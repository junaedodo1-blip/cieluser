The Step-by-Step Playbook

Click any prompt box to instantly copy the exact formula to your clipboard.

Module 01 Slide 1/8
Your brand needs to tell a story.
Why origin stories fail, and how customer transformations build billion-dollar brands.
Use reference style, 50mm clean lighting, razor-sharp typography for: "Your brand needs to tell a story.".
Module 02 Slide 2/8
Most brands tell a story about themselves.
Founder journey. Company values. How it all started.
  • Founders spend 10 minutes talking about their childhood garage ideas
  • Audiences scroll past self-centered origin timelines
  • People do not buy who you were; they buy who they become with you
Customers don't care how you got here. They care whether they see themselves in what happens next.
Module 03 Slide 3/8
The 10-Minute Origin Pitch Trap
A founder once opened a pitch with 10 minutes on his origin story.
  • The investors checked out by minute two
  • They wanted to know what changes for the customer, not for him
  • Shift the spotlight from your biography to the buyer’s transformation
RULE: The customer is always Luke Skywalker. You are Yoda.
Module 04 Slide 4/8
The story that actually sells isn't about your history.
It is a before and after transformation map.
  • Who someone was before they discovered you
  • Who they become after they experience your product
  • Every marketing touchpoint must sit somewhere on that line
TRANSFORMATION FORMULA: Before State (Frustrated & Stuck) → After State (Empowered & Transformed).
Module 05 Slide 5/8
Most 'brand stories' skip the tension entirely.
No conflict. No stakes. No reason to keep watching.
  • Without real tension, a story is just a dry chronological timeline
  • Show the obstacle, the frustration, the high-stakes friction
  • When tension is resolved, trust and loyalty are unlocked
NARRATIVE LAW: A story without a problem is just a timeline.
Module 06 Slide 6/8
Nobody remembers your founding story.
They remember the transformation they got to be part of.
  • 1. Hook: Agitate their current unresolved problem
  • 2. Mirror: Reflect the internal frustration they feel daily
  • 3. Guide: Offer the proven vehicle and actionable path forward
  • 4. Identity: Celebrate the new identity they step into
💾 SAVE THIS POST: The 4-Part Brand Transformation Architecture.
Module 07 Slide 7/8
The 3-Step Story Engine
How to write high-converting brand stories in under 5 minutes:
  • 1. Define the Old World (Pain, Friction, Antiquated methods)
  • 2. Introduce the Catalyst (The new vehicle, philosophy, or tool)
  • 3. Reveal the New World (Status elevation, speed, freedom)
