Module 02 Slide 2/8
why 90% of ai posters look cheap and messy.
Typing random words gives you chaotic layouts, blurry fonts, and plastic lighting.
  • Mistake 1: Starting with a blank prompt instead of a reference layout
  • Mistake 2: Asking for too many elements at once (visual clutter)
  • Mistake 3: Zero grid structure or typographic hierarchy
RULE: Great design is not about more prompt words. It is about strict layout rules.
Module 03 Slide 3/8
step 1: pick a grid reference layout.
Never start from scratch. Upload a graphic poster you love to guide the structure.
  • Choose a poster with bold stacked typography and clean grid lines
  • Let Nano Banana analyze the spacing, fonts, and color contrast
  • Keep the background minimal (off-white graph grid or deep black)
PRO TIP: Reference images act as the blueprint for your typography.
Module 04 Slide 4/8
step 2: the exact copy-paste prompt formula.
Paste this exact prompt structure into Nano Banana:
  • 1. "Use this image as reference for composition and typographic layout."
  • 2. "Swap main text to: [YOUR TITLE]. Archivo Black + Playfair Serif."
  • 3. "Lighting: 50mm clean prime portrait, soft raking key light, 35mm film grain."
PROMPT: "Use reference style, add 50mm macro lighting, razor-sharp typography, 4:5 vertical poster."
Module 05 Slide 5/8
step 3: add retro stickers & pastel highlights.
This is what gives the poster that high-end editorial agency aesthetic.
  • Add a soft pastel pink or lime highlighter strip behind the main keyword
  • Layer small speech bubbles with mini descriptive notes
  • Drop 3D micro icons (cherries, lightning, retro mouse cursors) near the letters
SECRET: Floating micro-stickers turn flat text into an interactive artwork.
Module 06 Slide 6/8
the saveable poster cheat sheet.
Save this slide for your next product poster creation:
  • 🎯 Canvas Size: 1080 x 1350 (4:5 vertical ratio)
  • 🎨 Colors: Off-white graph paper (#F9F9F7) + Black text + Neon lime/pink
  • 🔤 Typography: Extra Bold Sans Serif + Italic Display Serif (stacked tightly)
  • ✨ Finish: Raking side key light, lifted shadows, 35mm film grain
