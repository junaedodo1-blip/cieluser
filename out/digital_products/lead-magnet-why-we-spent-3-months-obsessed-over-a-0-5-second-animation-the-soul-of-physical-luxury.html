Module 02 Slide 2/8
The commodity trap: why 99% of AI visuals feel hollow.
Today, AI makes visual spectacle easy and cheap.
  • Cheap 3D renders lack weight, friction, and tactile human connection
  • Audiences scroll past generic glossy commercials without feeling anything
  • When everything looks perfect, nothing feels real or valuable
LAW OF SOUL: Spectacle without emotional stakes is forgotten in 3 seconds.
Module 03 Slide 3/8
Sensory physics: capturing real material weight.
Luxury is felt through physical resistance, gravity, and micro-texture.
  • Fluted glass perfume bottles refracting raw morning light
  • Heavy tailored Abyss Black wool catching natural directional shadows
  • Authentic 35mm prime lens distortion and fine lived-in surface pores
RULE 1: Physical materiality — viscosity, fabric weave, glass refraction — creates the emotional anchor.
Module 04 Slide 4/8
The 3-Act narrative spine behind every post.
Story is the only defensible commercial moat in an AI era.
  • Act I (The Truth): Unspoken human tension or cultural friction
  • Act II (The Struggle): Sensory discovery and suspended zero-G float
  • Act III (The Resolve): The physical object lands as the emotional catalyst
NARRATIVE FRAMEWORK: Tension creates attention. The physical object delivers the transformation.
Module 05 Slide 5/8
Auteur lighting: rejecting the commercial studio gloss.
Real cinema lighting embraces deep shadow wells and natural raking light.
  • Abyss Black (#0A0A0C) shadow wells create dramatic negative space
  • Directional raking light sculpts form without plastic AI sheen
  • Subtle volumetric mist and 35mm grain add cinematic depth
LIGHTING LAW: Deep shadows give light its authority and prestige.
