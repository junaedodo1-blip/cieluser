Module 02 Slide 2/8
why clean studio product renders are invisible.
When everything is clean, perfect, and isolated on a white screen, your brain ignores it.
  • Studio renders look like ordinary catalog advertisements
  • Our brains are wired to notice things that look out of place in the real world
  • Putting luxury inside brutalist concrete streets creates instant thumb-stopping contrast
RULE: Contrast creates curiosity, and curiosity creates millions of organic shares.
Module 03 Slide 3/8
step 1: the 10x impossible scale rule.
Take one small physical luxury item and scale it up to the size of a building or bus.
  • A 5-inch lipstick turned into a 20-foot city transit bus
  • A luxury handbag floating down the Seine river like a giant boat
  • Keep the material, leather grain, and stitching 100% physically authentic
SCALE RULE: Extreme surreal scale + 100% real physical texture = viral magic.
Module 04 Slide 4/8
step 2: raw brutalist urban environments.
Anchor the impossible object inside mundane, everyday physical architecture.
  • Paris cobblestone streets, London rainy crosswalks, brutalist concrete tunnels
  • Include real background elements: pedestrians walking, streetlights, asphalt reflections
  • The contrast between gritty streets and immaculate luxury creates high prestige
ENVIRONMENT RULE: The more ordinary the street, the more shocking the luxury object.
Module 05 Slide 5/8
step 3: handheld camera & bystander angle.
Shoot the scene from a realistic human perspective, not a floating drone.
  • Eye-level handheld camera with subtle natural shake
  • Casual street bystander angle makes the scene feel like a real iPhone recording
  • When viewers think "Is that real?!", they comment, share, and tag their friends
PERSPECTIVE RULE: Bystander angle turns a CGI render into a trending viral moment.
